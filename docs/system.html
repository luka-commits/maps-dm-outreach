Four DM strategies pick two; the table splits your leads and counts the replies

An opener is one line about the business's latest Instagram post. Claude drafts it, you check it. Highlighted parts are filled in per business.

1 · Area question

every channel
Hi, do you also cover Headingley?

A short, easy question to answer. Your offer comes in message two.

2 · Work done upfront

Instagram, with opener
Hey Sam, loved the chapel roof in your last post. I've already improved your Google profile so you can overtake Northside Roofing. Want it?

You do a piece of your own service first, here a better Google profile. The competitor named is the top-rated one in their town.

3 · Ask first

Instagram, with opener
that chapel roof came out lovely had a look at your google listing, want the two things i'd change?

The tips go only to people who say yes.

4 · Bigger free offer

Instagram, with opener
that chapel roof came out lovely i help roofers get found online. cool if i send you some fixes upfront?

Like 3, but offers more. Shows whether curiosity or free work wins.

No reply rates yet. The kit measures yours per strategy; below about 30 sends each, a difference is mostly chance. Your service and promise go in during setup. Examples are shortened; full templates are in strategies/.

Start

Setup, about 20 minutes

  1. Download the repo, open it in Claude Code, say “set up”.
  2. Claude installs the rest and checks each step.
  3. You: create a free Modal account and log in once.
  4. You, optional: paste an Apify key.
  5. Claude asks your name, offer and two strategies, then runs a test for under $0.30.

Every day

  1. Pull 10 to 20 leads. Claude drafts the openers; you check them.
  2. Click a channel: the profile opens and the message is copied. Paste, send, tick.
  3. Mark who replied. The table compares the two strategies.
