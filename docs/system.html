Automatable Community kit · Maps DM Outreach

A niche and a country in.
A DM-ready list of local businesses out.

The kit searches Google Maps postcode by postcode, reads every business website for its Instagram and Facebook, drops dead accounts, and puts the rest into a local outreach table. You send each DM by hand; the table tells you who is next, copies the message and tracks which strategy gets replies.

$ python run.py --country GB --niche roofer --region "West Yorkshire"

One real probe run

GB, “roofer”, 20 postcode cells in West Yorkshire, 9 November 2026. Total cost $0.26.

Why so many businesses drop out
  • Searching by postcode finds about ten times more businesses than one search for the whole county, because Google stops at about 60 results per search. Neighbouring cells overlap, so hits are deduplicated.
  • Too small means no website or fewer reviews than import.min_reviews (default 5). Both are settings.
  • No DM channel means neither Instagram nor Facebook was linked on the website. Those businesses cannot be messaged with this kit.
  • Not the niche catches suppliers and neighbouring trades that Google returns for the search.

Setup, guided by Claude Code

Open the folder in Claude Code and say “set up”. It runs setup/check.py after every step and only stops where you have to click or paste something yourself. About 20 minutes.

  1. Python environment Claude

    Creates .venv and installs two packages. Needs Python 3.10+.

  2. App Claude

    npm install in app/. Needs Node 22.13+, which ships SQLite.

  3. Modal account you

    Free sign-up at modal.com, then modal setup logs you in through the browser. Runs the Maps scraper.

  4. Apify token you

    Optional. Free account at apify.com; paste the API token into .env yourself. Powers the activity check and opener posts.

  5. Your settings Claude asks

    Your name, timezone, what you offer, and which two DM strategies to test first. Saved in outreach.config.json.

  6. Probe run Claude

    20 postcode cells in a region you pick, about ten cents. Then the table opens at localhost:4319.

Four DM strategies to split-test

Activate two. The table assigns each lead one of them, balanced, and the Analytics page compares reply rates per strategy and channel. Highlighted parts are filled in per lead. Examples use an invented business.

Strategy 1 · no opener

Area question

Hi, do you also cover Headingley?

A question any owner answers in two seconds, because it sounds like a job. The reply opens the conversation; you make the offer in message two.

all channels needs a neighbour town, computed on import
Strategy 2 · opener

Personal opener + done-for-you offer

Hey Sam, didn't know you also do heritage slate repairs, the chapel roof in your last post 😮 I checked your visibility in Leeds and Headingley and optimised your Google profile so you can get past Northside Roofing. You only need to copy it. My promise: ten new jobs in 60 days, free for you. Can I send you the profile?

The work is already done and costs nothing to accept. The competitor is the business with the most reviews in the same town.

Instagram needs an opener from their posts
Strategy 3 · opener

Post comment + permission question

that chapel roof on your last post came out lovely 😍 had a look at your google listing while i was there, want the two things i'd change?

The first message spends nothing. The finding is message two, sent only to people who said yes.

Instagram needs an opener
Strategy 4 · opener

Post comment + upfront audit offer

Hey Sam, that chapel roof on your last post came out lovely 😍 i help roofers get found online. cool if i send you your google profile, website and seo fixes upfront?

Same opening as strategy 3, a different ask. Run 3 against 4 to learn whether curiosity or free work gets more replies.

Instagram needs an opener

The opener is the only line a person writes. Claude drafts it from their last six posts with the dm-opener skill, and you read every one before sending. Rules for good openers: strategies/README.md.

A day of outreach

  1. Pull a batch of 10 to 20 leads. Branches that share one profile get one DM.
  2. Write openers for leads whose strategy needs one, or switch them to the area question.
  3. Work every channel per lead the same day: click opens the profile and copies the message, you paste and send, then tick.
  4. Set stages as replies arrive: Contacted, Replied, Asset sent, Meeting, Not interested.

Wait for about 30 sent per strategy before calling a winner, and change one thing at a time.

What it deliberately does not do

No automated sending.Every DM goes out by hand from your own account. Automated DMs get accounts restricted and read like automation.
No email.The kit is built for social DMs on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn only.
No cloud database.Leads stay in data/leads.db on your machine and are never committed.
